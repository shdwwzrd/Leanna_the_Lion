LEANNA THE LION - Game Boy (DMG) version, v1.0
by Neo Retro Games   https://neoretrogames.itch.io/leanna-the-lion

Leanna may be a lion, but she knows what she wants: fresh meat from her favorite
restaurant. The zoo had to close.

HOW TO PLAY
  D-pad UP / DOWN   change lane
  A or B            leap forward (clears puddles, then you drop back)
  START             play (on the title screen)

  Eat people for points: elder, child, adult, monkey, zoo keeper.
  Hot sauce doubles your score for 15 seconds.
  Puddles and monkey poo make you slide. Elephants and cages end the game,
  and so do tranquilizer darts from the zoo keepers and the truck.

TITLE MENU
  LEFT / RIGHT moves the cursor, A selects: PLAY, SCORES, SOUND on/off.

TOP SCORES
  The best 8 scores are saved with 3 letter initials. When you make the list,
  UP/DOWN change a letter, LEFT/RIGHT move between letters, A confirms.

RUNNING IT
  Leanna.gb is a 32 KB Game Boy ROM (MBC1 + battery RAM for the saved scores).
  Works in Game Boy emulators (mGBA, SameBoy, BGB, Emulicious...) and on flash
  carts (EverDrive, EMS, GB Flash...). Tested on an original DMG.
  The save file is created next to the ROM by most emulators (Leanna.sav).
